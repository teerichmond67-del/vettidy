Support

Need a hand?

VetTidy is a young app built and maintained by a small team, so support is hands-on rather than a ticket queue.

Email us

The fastest way to reach us is support@vettidy.com. Include your account email and, if it's about a specific pet or record, which pet — it helps us find your data faster.

Common requests

  • Account or data deletion — email us; self-service deletion isn't built into the app yet, so we handle these by hand.
  • Lost access to a shared Pack — if you're a Caregiver or Sitter who lost access, ask the Pack owner to re-invite you from the Caregivers tab.
  • Billing / subscription issues — Premium purchases are billed and managed by the App Store or Google Play, so refunds and billing questions are best handled through them directly; we're glad to help if you're stuck.

See also our Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.
