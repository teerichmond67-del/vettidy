FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you'd want to know before trusting VetTidy with a pet's records.

What is VetTidy?

An app that keeps a pet's profile, documents, vaccination history, medications, and weight in one place — built so casual memory-keeping and rigorous medical record-keeping don't require two different apps.

Is VetTidy free?

The core is free, forever: unlimited pet profiles, the document vault, and vaccination reminders. Premium is a paid subscription that adds household sharing, medication tracking, weight trend charts, and PDF export.

What species does VetTidy support?

Any species. There's no hardcoded list of dogs and cats — profiles work the same for a bird, a reptile, or anything else you keep records for.

Can I share a pet's records with family or a sitter?

Yes, on Premium. Invite someone with full Caregiver access or read-only Sitter access, and everyone in the household sees updates in real time — no manual sync step.

Does VetTidy connect to my vet's practice-management system?

No. Your records stay your own — exportable and shareable on your terms, not tied to any single clinic's software.

When can I download VetTidy?

VetTidy is still in development and isn't published to the App Store or Google Play yet. Get in touch if you'd like to hear when it launches.
